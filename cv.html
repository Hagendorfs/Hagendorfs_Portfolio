Bio nr.2

Hagendorfs is currently involved in an ongoing collaborative project initiated by the Glass Department of the Estonian Academy of Arts, the MA POST programme at the Art Academy of Latvia, Līvāni City, the Līvāni Glass Centre, and MUD Lab. His practice has also led him to become a member of the Bad Ideas Collective, supported by DOMOBAAL. He continues his collaboration with artist Līva Priedīte in an upcoming two-person exhibition at DOM Gallery. In 2026, he received an Academic Achievement Award for his studies in the MA POST programme at the Art Academy of Latvia.
